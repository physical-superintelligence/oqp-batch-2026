# Fidelity: Open Quantum Problem 12 and the Lean statements

This file maps the text of Open Quantum Problem 12 ([problem statement](OQP_STATEMENT.md)) and the results of the
full manuscript of 21 October 2026 to the definitions and theorems in
[`Challenge.lean`](../Challenge.lean). The theorem and equation numbers follow the
[full manuscript](../paper/README.md). All library names below are in the namespace
`VacuumBellSeparation`; the six theorems checked by Comparator are in `VacuumBellSeparation.OQP12`.

## The formal statements

| Lean theorem (`OQP12.…`) | What it says |
|---|---|
| `no_chsh_violation_far_apart` (headline) | For every `m > 0`, bounded `O₁, O₂` and spacelike `a`, there is `T₀` such that for all `t ≥ T₀`, in every vacuum Weyl system of mass `m`, all self-adjoint contractions `A₀, A₁ ∈ 𝔄(O₁)`, `B₀, B₁ ∈ 𝔄(O₂ + t a)` satisfy `‖⟨Ω, (A₀(B₀ + B₁) + A₁(B₀ − B₁)) Ω⟩‖ ≤ 2`. This is Corollary 1.2 of the paper in CHSH form. |
| `theorem_1_1` | Theorem 1.1 of the paper: one `C_* > 2` such that `β_m(R, L) = 1` for all `m, R > 0` and `L ≥ C_* R`, for the double cones of radius `R` centred at the origin and at `(0, L𝐞₁)` (centres at spatial distance `L`, gap `L − 2R`). |
| `corollary_1_2` | Corollary 1.2 of the paper: for bounded `O₁, O₂` and spacelike `a`, the vacuum Bell correlation of `𝔄(O₁)` and `𝔄(O₂ + t a)` equals `1` for all large `t`. As in the paper, the second region is translated; translating the first by `t a` instead is the same as translating the second by `−t a`, which the statement also covers (`−a` is spacelike). |
| `freeFieldVacuum_exists` | For every `m > 0` some vacuum Weyl system exists in a Hilbert space `H : Type`, the universe over which the other statements quantify (non-vacuity). |
| `no_chsh_violation_far_apart_uniform` | For bounded `O₁, O₂` there is `D` such that the CHSH inequality holds for `𝔄(O₁)` and `𝔄(O₂ + (0, 𝐱))` for every spatial vector `𝐱` with `‖𝐱‖ ≥ D`, in every direction. Not a numbered result of the paper: it follows from Theorem 1.1, a spatial reflection and isotony (`FreeField/OQP12.lean`). |
| `localAlgebra_open_nonabelian` | Sanity check: for every nonempty open `O`, `𝔄(O)` contains `A, B` with `AB ≠ BA`. |

In `theorem_1_1` and `corollary_1_2` the vacuum Bell correlation is written out as
`sSup {v | ∃ A₀ A₁ B₀ B₁, … ∧ v = ½ ‖⟨Ω, (A₀(B₀ + B₁) + A₁(B₀ − B₁)) Ω⟩‖}`, the supremum of the CHSH values
over self-adjoint contractions of the two local algebras. The library defines this quantity as
`bellCorrelation (vectorState Ω _) 𝔄 𝔅` (paper, equation (2.4)) and proves the two forms equal
(`bellCorrelation_vacuum_eq_sSup`, `FreeField/OQP12.lean`); its statements `Theorem_1_1` and `Corollary_1_2`
(`FreeField/Statements.lean`) use the named form.

[docs/PAPER_TO_LEAN.md](PAPER_TO_LEAN.md) maps every numbered statement, equation and table of the paper to its Lean counterpart in the library, where there is one, and says how far the checks reached each declaration.

## Phrase by phrase

| Text of the problem | Lean |
|---|---|
| "a massive scalar free relativistic Bose field" | `FreeFieldVacuum m H` for every `m > 0`: the real scalar field in 3+1 dimensions, with Weyl operators `W(F)` of real test functions on `Spacetime = ℝ × ℝ³` and the Weyl relations of the one-particle inner product `oneParticleInner m` (measure `d³p / (2(2π)³ ω_m(𝐩))`, `ω_m(𝐩) = √(m² + ‖𝐩‖²)`). |
| "vacuum" (fluctuations, correlations) | The vacuum vector `V.vacuum` of a vacuum Weyl system: a cyclic unit vector with `⟨Ω, W(F) Ω⟩ = exp(−Re⟨κF, κF⟩/2)`. Every statement quantifies over all vacuum Weyl systems in all Hilbert spaces `H : Type`. Such systems exist in `Type` (`freeFieldVacuum_exists`). The library also proves, outside the six theorems that Comparator checks, that any two of them, in any universes, are related by a unitary that carries vacuum, Weyl operators, local algebras and vacuum expectations (`FreeFieldVacuum.exists_unitary_equiv`, `FreeField/Uniqueness.lean`), and builds one on the symmetric Fock space over `L²(ℝ³, d𝔪_m)` (`literalFockVacuum`, `FreeField/LiteralFockVacuumConstruction.lean`; equivalence `FreeFieldVacuum.exists_literalFock_equiv`, `FreeField/LiteralFockVacuum.lean`). So each statement says the same as the corresponding statement about the Fock vacuum, and quantifying over `H : Type` loses nothing. |
| "localization regions" | `localAlgebra V O`: the von Neumann algebra generated by the `W(F)` with `tsupport F ⊆ O` (closed support in `O`), `F` real, smooth (`ContDiff ℝ (⊤ : ℕ∞)`, that is `C^∞`; a bare `⊤` would be the analytic order in this Mathlib) and compactly supported; the standard local net of the free field. `O` is any set of spacetime points. If `O` has empty interior, the only test function with closed support in `O` is `0` and `𝔄(O) = ℂ·1`, so the statements hold trivially there; every region with nonempty interior has a noncommutative algebra (`localAlgebra_open_nonabelian` and isotony, `localAlgebra_mono`). |
| "the CHSH-Bell inequalities ... observables" | Headline and uniform version: `‖⟨Ω, (A₀(B₀ + B₁) + A₁(B₀ − B₁)) Ω⟩‖ ≤ 2` for all self-adjoint contractions `Aᵢ ∈ 𝔄(O₁)`, `Bⱼ ∈ 𝔄(O₂')`; `theorem_1_1`, `corollary_1_2`: the supremum of the CHSH values `½ ‖⟨Ω, …⟩‖` equals `1`. Observables are self-adjoint operators of norm at most 1 (spectrum in `[−1, 1]`), including the two-valued `±1` observables; two settings with two outcomes on each side, as in the CHSH inequality of the problem. The supremum is the Summers–Werner normalization `β` with classical bound `1`; `β = 1` is equivalent to the headline inequality (the set of CHSH values contains `1` and is bounded by `4`: `one_le_bellCorrelation`, `bddAbove_chshValues`; for commuting algebras `β ≤ √2`: `bellCorrelation_le_sqrt_two`). The vacuum expectation is taken without any selection of outcomes (no local filtering before the test). |
| "spacelike separated" | `a.1 ^ 2 < ‖a.2‖ ^ 2` (strictly spacelike `a`), and spatial vectors `(0, 𝐱)` in the uniform version. For bounded regions and large `t`, `O₁` and `O₂ + t a` are spacelike separated; this follows from boundedness. |
| "regions arbitrarily far apart" | `O₂` moved by `t a`, `t ≥ T₀`, for a fixed spacelike `a` (`no_chsh_violation_far_apart`, `corollary_1_2`); `O₂` moved by any spatial `𝐱` with `‖𝐱‖ ≥ D`, uniformly in the direction (`no_chsh_violation_far_apart_uniform`); double cones `D_R` and `D_R + (0, L𝐞₁)` with `L ≥ C_* R` (`theorem_1_1`). The regions are bounded; the thresholds `T₀` and `D` depend on the regions (and on `m` and `a`). |
| "Decide whether some (necessarily small) violation ... is possible" | The answer is no for the families above: `no_chsh_violation_far_apart` is exactly the negation of "for some bounded `O₁, O₂` and spacelike `a`, a CHSH violation occurs for arbitrarily large `t`". |

## Conventions and modelling choices

- **States.** The vacuum state is the vector state `T ↦ ⟨Ω, T Ω⟩`, written out as an inner product in the
  Challenge. The library's `State H` is a positive normalized functional on all bounded operators of `H`.
- **Signature and coordinates.** Points are `(x⁰, 𝐱) ∈ ℝ × ℝ³`; `doubleCone a R = {x : |x⁰ − a⁰| + ‖𝐱 − 𝐚‖ < R}`;
  the norm on `Spacetime` used in `Bornology.IsBounded` is the product (max) norm, so "bounded" has its usual
  meaning.
- **Weyl relations.** `W(F) W(G) = exp(−i Im⟨κF, κG⟩) W(F + G)` with `W(F) = e^{iφ(F)}`, so
  `[φ(F), φ(G)] = 2i Im⟨κF, κG⟩`, the Pauli–Jordan commutator of the free field.
- **Test functions.** Real, `C^∞`, compactly supported, on spacetime.
- **Commuting algebras.** The paper defines `β` in (2.4) for commuting von Neumann algebras; the supremum in the statements is the same expression, defined for any pair of algebras. The library proves microcausality, that the local algebras of two unit double cones whose centres are at distance `s > 2` commute (`locality`, `FreeField/Microcausality/Edges.lean`). Tsirelson's bound `√2` holds for commuting algebras (`bellCorrelation_le_sqrt_two`).
- **Hypotheses.** The hypotheses are the data of the problem (a mass `m > 0`, two bounded regions, a
  direction). Comparator permits exactly the axioms `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice`.

## How the Challenge definitions are kept faithful to the library

`Challenge.lean` copies the definitions its statements use, verbatim and in order, from the beginning of
`FreeField/Defs.lean` (`Spacetime` to `e₁`), between `-- verbatim:` markers; `scripts/check-challenge-definitions.py`
checks that the block occurs character for character in the library, and Comparator checks that the
constants are identical. Lean names the auxiliary proof constants inside definitions (`_proof_n`) per module
and reuses an earlier one of the same type, so the copy is one contiguous initial segment of one library
module; for the same reason the Bell correlation (from `Bell/Defs.lean`) is written out in the statements.
