# Paper to Lean

This note maps the numbered statements, equations and tables of the full manuscript to the
declarations of the Lean library `VacuumBellSeparation` in this repository. The source is
the version of 21 October 2026, "The vacuum Bell correlation of the massive free scalar field attains the classical bound for bounded regions beyond a finite separation" (paper in preparation; see
[manuscript correspondence](../paper/README.md)); its theorem, section and equation numbers are those of the long paper version 3.1
of 12 September 2026. The Lean files are those of the checked commit
`6a91bb4b256579eb39f6a5f08f2cb0c1e9bc94e5`. The letter of 22 October 2026 presents
the full manuscript's Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.2 as Theorem 1 and Corollary 1.

Lean names are written in full. File paths are relative to the directory `VacuumBellSeparation/`,
except `Challenge.lean` and `Solution.lean`, which are at the root of the repository. Each table
row names the paper item, its Lean counterpart, and how the two correspond.

it:

- Comparator: one of the six theorems.
- replayed: used, directly or through other declarations, in the proofs of the six theorems, so it
  lies in the dependency closure that the kernel replayed and that the axiom audit covered.
- compiled: compiled by the fresh check; it lies outside that dependency closure.

## Reading the Lean statements

- The statements hold for every vacuum Weyl system `VacuumBellSeparation.FreeFieldVacuum`
  (FreeField/Defs.lean; replayed) of mass m in every complex Hilbert space: unitary Weyl operators
  of real spacetime test functions with the Weyl relations of the one-particle inner product, and a
  unit cyclic vacuum vector with the vacuum characteristic function of Section 2.1. Such a system
  exists for every m > 0 (`VacuumBellSeparation.OQP12.freeFieldVacuum_exists` (Challenge.lean and
  Solution.lean; Comparator)), and the Bell correlation does not depend on the choice
  (`VacuumBellSeparation.FreeFieldVacuum.freeFieldBell_eq` (FreeField/Uniqueness.lean; replayed)).
- Local algebras are generated by the Weyl operators of spacetime test functions supported in the
  region (`VacuumBellSeparation.localAlgebra` (FreeField/Defs.lean; replayed)); the time-zero
  description (2.3) is a theorem.
- The states of Section 3 are states on all bounded operators of the Hilbert space
  (`VacuumBellSeparation.State` (Bell/Defs.lean; replayed)); the vacuum state is
  `VacuumBellSeparation.vectorState` (FreeField/Defs.lean; replayed).
- Quantities that the paper allows to be infinite, the event comparison constants and the
  sandwiched Rényi quantity, take values in [0, ∞].
- Where the paper fixes a constant only by its existence, the Lean statement quantifies over it
  existentially or gives an explicit number that the proof establishes.
