# Open Quantum Problems, Problem 27: "The power of CGLMP inequalities"

- List: Open Quantum Problems, IQOQI Vienna
- Problem: 27, "The power of CGLMP inequalities", codified by R. Gill
- Page: <https://oqp.iqoqi.oeaw.ac.at/the-power-of-cglmp-inequalities/>
- Snapshot: text as transcribed on 8 September 2026; the citation markers of the original are
  replaced by the keys listed below.

## Statement (verbatim)

> In the setting of Problem 26, consider especially the case (N,M,K)=(2,2,d).
>
> **Problem 27.A.** Show that every face of the local polytope C, which is not already contained in a
> face of the no-signalling polytope P is of CGLMP type, i.e., an inequality of the form first written
> out in [CGLMP], but possibly lifted from lower dimensions by fusing together some outcomes.
>
> **Problem 27.B.** Numerically, the observables maximally violating the CGLMP inequality on a
> maximally entangled state are of a very specific form [DKZ], involving measurements in computational
> basis, transformed by only discrete Fourier transformation and diagonal unitaries [CGLMP]. Show that
> this is necessarily the case. Show also that these measurements realize the highest resistance of
> violation to noise, and the best discrimination against classical realism in the sense of
> Kullback–Leibler divergence [VDGG].

Here N, M and K are the numbers of parties, settings per party and outcomes per measurement; Problem 26
of the same list concerns Bell inequalities in this general setting.

## Cited works

- [CGLMP] D. Collins, N. Gisin, N. Linden, S. Massar and S. Popescu, "Bell inequalities for arbitrarily
  high-dimensional systems", Phys. Rev. Lett. 88, 040404 (2002), arXiv:quant-ph/0106024.
- [DKZ] T. Durt, D. Kaszlikowski and M. Żukowski, "Violations of local realism with quantum systems
  described by N-dimensional Hilbert spaces up to N=16", Phys. Rev. A 64, 024101 (2001),
  arXiv:quant-ph/0101084.
- [VDGG] W. van Dam, R. D. Gill and P. D. Grünwald, "The statistical strength of nonlocality proofs",
  IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 51, 2812–2835 (2005), arXiv:quant-ph/0307125.
